4Compare September and October

September: 234,567. October: 234,576.

Answer: C, September had fewer passengers than October

Steps

  1. Compare digits from the left. 2, 3, 4, 5 are the same in both numbers.
  2. The first difference is in the tens place: September has 6 tens, October has 7 tens.
  3. 6 < 7, so 234,567 < 234,576. September is smaller.

7Find the difference: 82.5 − 51.53

Answer: A, 30.97

Steps

  1. Line up the decimal points. Write 82.5 as 82.50 so both numbers have two decimal places.
  2. Subtract: 82.50 − 51.53.
  3. Hundredths: 0 − 3 needs a borrow, so 10 − 3 = 7. The 5 tenths become 4.
  4. Tenths: 4 − 5 needs a borrow, so 14 − 5 = 9. The 2 ones become 1.
  5. Ones: 1 − 1 = 0. Tens: 8 − 5 = 3.
  6. Result: 30.97

Check by adding back: 30.97 + 51.53 = 82.50 ✔

8Multiply: 8 × 900

Answer: D, 7,200

Steps

  1. Ignore the zeros for now: 8 × 9 = 72.
  2. 900 has two zeros, so put two zeros after 72.
  3. Result: 7,200

Another way: 900 is 9 hundreds. 8 × 9 hundreds = 72 hundreds = 7,200.

9Javier's flower arrangements

7 arrangements, 15 flowers in each. How many flowers in total?

Answer: 105 flowers

Steps

  1. Equal groups means multiply: 7 × 15.
  2. Break 15 into 10 + 5.
    • 7 × 10 = 70
    • 7 × 5 = 35
  3. Add: 70 + 35 = 105.

Write in the box: 105 flowers
